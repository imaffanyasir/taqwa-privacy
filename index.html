Taqwa — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

Taqwa is an offline Islamic companion app for prayer times, Quran reading, reminders and home-screen widgets. It is designed so that your personal information stays on your device.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal data.

Information processed on your device

  • Location (optional). If you allow location access, your approximate or precise coordinates are read once to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. They are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. You can instead choose a city or enter coordinates manually, and the app works fully without location permission.
  • Your content and settings. Bookmarks, notes, reading progress, Khatm plans, tasbeeh counts and preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Sensors. The compass (magnetometer and accelerometer) is used live on the Qibla screen and nothing is recorded.

Permissions

  • Location: optional; used only as described above.
  • Notifications: to alert you at prayer times and for reminders you turn on.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so prayer alerts arrive on time.
  • Full-screen notifications: only if you enable a full-screen prayer alarm.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to play the adhan at prayer time, which you can stop from the notification.
  • Run at startup: to restore your prayer alarms after the phone restarts.

Backups and exports

The app does not use cloud backup. You may choose to export your data to a file using Settings › Privacy & data. That file is saved where you choose and is not accessed by us. You can erase all app data at any time in the same screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Third parties

No third-party services receive data from the app. Location may be obtained through your device's system location service, which is governed by your device provider's privacy policy. If you name a place from GPS coordinates, your device's built-in geocoder may be used and may be subject to its provider's policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
